BUILT FOR CONTROL

Simple tools. Clear decisions.

CallGuard uses Android's call-screening role and your locally stored rules.

✓

Trusted contacts

Review and import phone numbers from your device Contacts app into your VIP allow-list. Call history and SMS are not used for importing.

⊘

Block by choice

Maintain your own block list. Explicit VIP allow rules take priority over block rules.

◷

Screening activity

Review recent CallGuard screening events. This is app activity, not a complete copy of the phone's system call log.

♢

VIP ring override

With the required permissions, an allowed VIP call can switch Silent or Vibrate to Normal, then restore the prior mode.

FIRST-TIME CONFIGURATION

Set up CallGuard correctly.

Android requires separate system permissions. Installing the APK alone does not activate call screening.

01

Enable call screening

Open CallGuard and tap Enable call screening. Approve the Android Call Screening role request. Return to the app and confirm the status says Screening Active.

02

Import VIP contacts

Tap Review and import contacts and grant Contacts permission. Numbers are read from the device address book, not the call log.

03

Enable VIP ring override

Turn on the VIP ring override. Grant Contacts and Phone state permissions, then enable CallGuard under Android's Do Not Disturb access settings.

04

Test with DND off

Set the phone to Silent or Vibrate, make sure Do Not Disturb is OFF, and call from a number listed as VIP. The original ringer mode should be restored after the call.

Important: VIP ring override does not bypass Do Not Disturb. Android manufacturer restrictions and device-specific call handling can affect the result. If screening status is not Active, the call-screening role has not been granted.

PRIVACY IS A PRODUCT DECISION

Your rules belong
to your device.

CallGuard's MVP uses Android's call-screening framework and a deterministic allow-first policy. Allow/block rules are encrypted in app-private storage with Android Keystore-backed AES-GCM.

Unknown or unavailable caller IDs are allowed by default. Recent screening events are stored locally with masked numbers. The activity list is not the system call log.

Contact import requires your permission and reads phone numbers from the device Contacts provider. The app does not import SMS or call history.
