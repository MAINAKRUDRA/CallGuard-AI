PRIVACY IS A PRODUCT DECISION

Your rules belong
to your device.

CallGuard AI's current MVP uses Android's call-screening framework and a deterministic allow-first policy. Your allow/block rules stay in app-private storage, protected with Android Keystore encryption.

Unknown or unavailable caller IDs are allowed by default. Call history stores masked numbers locally; the history database is not encrypted in this phase.
